🐎 The Legend of the Stallion 🐎

In the beginning, there was Cape Town. And Cape Town looked upon the wind and the waves and the wild horses of the coast and said: "This is not enough." And so, from the union of Table Mountain and the Southern Ocean, a child was born who would make grown stallions bow their heads in reverence.

His name was Sebastian Chait. And he was magnificent.

Chapter I: The Foaling

Born under a blood moon in Cape Town, South Africa, young Sebastian displayed an unusual affinity for horses from the moment he could walk. Legend has it he spoke his first word not to his mother, but to a passing Thoroughbred. The word was "faster." He was eleven months old. The horse understood.

Chapter II: The Awakening

By age twelve, Sebastian was training riders of all ages, from children who could barely reach the stirrups to seasoned competitors who had forgotten what it meant to truly ride. Horses would line up voluntarily at the paddock gate when they heard his footsteps. Some say they still do, even when he's in a different country entirely.

Chapter III: The Unbridled Journey

Cape Town could not contain him. Like a wild stallion that refuses to be fenced, Sebastian galloped across continents, leaving a trail of swooning horses, envious riders, and at least one yacht captain who described him as "the most naturally gifted human I have ever witnessed mount anything."
